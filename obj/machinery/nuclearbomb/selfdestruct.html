update_ui_mode
()

Drops the "input time" step out of the keypad flow entirely.

The countdown is fixed to the announcement, so there is nothing to enter - the stock flow would just take a number and clamp it back to SELF_DESTRUCT_DURATION_SECONDS. Accepting the code takes the safety off itself and goes straight to the arming prompt.
