# Dogmos Credits

Dogmos is part of Meridian Rift, a Space Station 13 downstream of TG and Nova Sector.

The atmospheric port is based on Auxmos by Putnam3145:

- `https://github.com/Putnam3145/auxmos`

The Meridian-MCP tooling is an adaptation of dm-mcp by imcynic:

- `https://github.com/imcynic/dm-mcp`

The current integration, gameplay contracts, tests, documentation, and Kennel interface are
maintained in the Meridian Rift and Aphelion project repositories by their contributors and
maintainers. This page credits project lineages and repositories rather than assigning individual
authorship.
