SELF_DESTRUCT_BLAST_ECHO_PROBChance a secondary detonation is heard across the whole station rather than only nearby. Every blast reaching every player is one guaranteed sound each, and by the tail end that is a constant stream fighting the announcement for the client's voices.
SELF_DESTRUCT_BLAST_SOUND_PRIORITYPriority on the secondary detonation sounds, deliberately near the floor. If a client has to drop something when the blasts get dense, it should be a blast and never the announcement.
